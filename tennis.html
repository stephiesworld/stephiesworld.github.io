05 — Tennis

Tennis

Tennis found me later in life and became one of the most important teachers I've had — the sport is a mirror.

Every weakness shows up on the court before it shows up anywhere else. Learning to lose well, to stay present between points, to trust a swing you've hit ten thousand times — it's the inner game, and I keep chasing it.
