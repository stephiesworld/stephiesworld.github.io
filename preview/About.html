A short life,
kept in currents.

Between Shanghai and Texas, then New York because it reminded me of Shanghai — my first love. I work in tech, write quietly, and am forever open to recommendations.

I grew up between Shanghai (in Jing'An) and Texas (specifically, Spring, Texas of all places) and landed in New York because it reminded me of Shanghai, my first love. I work in tech and am writing a literary science fiction novel about an alien that falls in love with life on Earth.

Most of what interests me is learning how different people see the world — through art, music, film, literature, philosophy, and the stories they carry with them. There are few things better than a long run with music you love, a warm day, or anywhere water meets land — though I'd rather be near it than in it.

I like tennis, boating, laughing, and making things. I'm always reading multiple books at once and am forever open to recommendations.
