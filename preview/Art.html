Fondation
Louis Vuitton

Only one exhibit at a time. Well-curated. But what I love most is the approach.

The art is almost secondary. Each level traces the arc of the artist's life — the beginning, the middle, the end — with the inspirations and catalysts that shaped them along the way.

You leave understanding not just what someone made, but why they made it.
