iv. / present

Amazon

post · little bun

As challenging as Amazon is known for being, it wasn't anything close to how hard you are on yourself as a founder.

I get to work with businesses to sell and grow, and I've loved learning the systems inside Amazon's world — the logistics, the scale, the invisible infrastructure most shoppers never see.

iii. / founder

Took everything I learned at Harry's and built a food startup. A big lesson in all the wrong things to do, honestly — but I wouldn't trade it.

"Founding teaches you how hard you can be on yourself before it teaches you anything else."
ii. / where I learned to build

Harry's

Built new products with incredible, fun, kind people. When I showed up to the office sick one day, a colleague said —

At the same time — a razor is five mini knives that people put on their face. Worth a little care.
