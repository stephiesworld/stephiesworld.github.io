Paris,
a field
guide.

A working list. Places I return to, things I learned to love slowly, and the small corners of the city that feel like they were made for a long afternoon with nowhere to be.
I. / museums

Where I keep
going back.

Paris is a city that rewards patience. These are the museums where I've spent the longest afternoons, and the ones I return to whenever I'm passing through.

iv entries
01
Fondation Louis Vuitton
My favorite museum anywhere. One exhibit at a time. The arc of an artist's life traced floor by floor — beginning, middle, end, and the inspirations that shaped them.
16e · bois de boulogne
02
Musée Rodin
A garden of sculptures, then the house. Read You Must Change Your Life before you go.
7e
03
Musée de l'Orangerie
The two oval rooms of water lilies. Show up early. Sit a while.
1er · tuileries
04
Musée d'Orsay
Renoir on the top floor. The clock window you've seen a thousand times in photos is better in person.
7e
