The Cabinet

ix entries · always growing
no. 001
language

The word "saudade"

Portuguese. The feeling of missing something you aren't sure ever existed. I think about this one constantly.

no. 002
physics

Each photon remembers
every path

Feynman's sum-over-histories. A photon travels every possible route between two points; what we see is the interference of all of them.

no. 003
number

Why the number 52
follows me

East 52nd Street, 52 Stories, 52 Rue du Bac. Somewhere between coincidence and a small conspiracy.

no. 004
biology

Octopus colorblindness

They can't see color — and yet they match their surroundings perfectly. Current theory: their skin itself senses light.

no. 005
philosophy

The ship of Theseus,
but for people

Every seven years, almost every cell in your body is replaced. At what point are you still the same person? (I don't think it's a body question.)

no. 006
sound

Two violins are louder
than one — barely

Ten violins aren't ten times louder than one. Sound doesn't add linearly; it adds in decibels. The whole orchestra is doing less than you think.

no. 007
astronomy

The light from
Proxima b

The nearest known exoplanet. Light from it takes four years to reach us. Whatever we see of it is four years old — a small letter from the past.

no. 008
habit

The first warm day
of spring

Everyone in New York spills into the parks at once. No plans. No announcement. An entire city agreeing to something without saying a word.

no. 009
writing

The book you're
almost ready to write

Is always the one you've been avoiding. The resistance is the signal. The thing you keep putting off is usually the thing worth doing.
