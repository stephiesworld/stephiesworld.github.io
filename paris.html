field guide

Paris

Where beauty is a necessity rather than a luxury. A few places I keep going back to.

A few universal rules: every boulangerie is the right boulangerie. Every coffee is fine. Every meal will probably be good. Every bookstore is an adventure. Beauty is mandatory in Paris, and that includes the shops. Very low floors, very high ceilings.

Fitness

Burning Bar

42 Avenue Raymond Poincaré · 16th arr.

Hot pilates, afrohouse music, mood lighting, French-speaking instructors. My favorite workout class in the entire world. I don't say that lightly.

Museums

Musée Rodin

77 Rue de Varenne · 7th arr.

One of the most peaceful museums in Paris, with a garden that does most of the heavy lifting. Plan to stay longer than you think.

Fondation Louis Vuitton

8 Avenue du Mahatma Gandhi · Bois de Boulogne

My favorite art museum I've ever visited. One exhibit at a time, each level tracing the arc of the artist's life: the inspirations, the catalysts, the turning points. You leave understanding why someone made the work as much as what it is.

Restaurants

Au P'tit Curieux

27 Rue de Sambre-et-Meuse · 10th arr.

My best friend Mackenzie and I have had multiple four-hour dinners here, sometimes twice in the same trip. The owner is funny and charming. One of those places you come back to without deciding to.

Cojean

28 Rue du Bac · 7th arr.

Healthy bowls clearly marked with protein content. If I lived in Paris full time, this would be my daily fast casual. Locations across the city.

Parks

Jardin du Luxembourg

6th arr.

The most beautiful park in the world, and I will stand by that. Tennis courts you can reserve in advance, which is just unfair to every other park in existence.

Shopping

Citypharma

26 Rue du Four · 6th arr.

The French pharmacy experience is non-negotiable. Go in without a list, leave with fifteen things you didn't know you needed.

From Future

Le Marais · multiple locations

My favorite sweater shop in the world (do you see the theme?). Delightful, colorful cashmere in the most charming little store. You'll wear it all winter.

Tennis

Roland-Garros

2 Avenue Gordon Bennett · 16th arr. · late May – early June

Iconic, beautiful, peaceful, and so, so clean. The red clay is legendary and the battles here are hard fought and won. Easy to get in and out from the center of Paris.

Explore

Rue du Bac

7th arr.

Walk the whole thing, from the Seine to Bon Marché. One of the most charming streets in Paris, and my first Paris address was number 52.

Saint-Germain-des-Prés

6th arr.

My favorite arrondissement. Little restaurants, famous shops, people hanging out. Just walk around.

The Seine at Sunset

Les Berges de la Seine

Go for a run here during sunset. One of the best running routes in the world, and yes, I am completely biased about this city.
