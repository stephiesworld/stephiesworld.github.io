The museum

Fondation Louis Vuitton

My favorite is the Fondation Louis Vuitton in Paris. One exhibit at a time, always well-curated — but what I love is the approach. The art is almost secondary. Each level traces the arc of an artist’s life — beginning, middle, end — with the inspirations and catalysts along the way. You leave understanding not just what someone made, but why.
