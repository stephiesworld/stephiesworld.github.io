Delphi

a knowledge base for the stocks people actually trade

live ↗

Why I made it

I wanted to understand the companies retail investors trade most. Robinhood’s AI-written summaries told me what happened, but not why it mattered. The clearest explanations I’d ever gotten came from a few people I know who can talk about a company like it’s someone they know.

What it does

Delphi is an extrapolation of those conversations. Pick any of the top stocks and see the market dynamics, the open questions, and what the company means for the world. Each one also gets a buy, hold, or sell view based on its 12-month forward guidance.

The ratings are my opinion, not investment advice.

Henry

the portal Amazon’s biggest sellers deserve

live ↗code ↗

Why I made it

A sample of what the world could look like if Amazon’s enterprise customers had the perfect portal. At Amazon I worked with the largest businesses selling on the platform, and I was surprised there was no one-stop shop that simply answered their questions.

What it does

Henry is that shop. It handles everyday P&L tasks, maps the competitive landscape including pricing, shows where margin goes, and gives instant supply chain help.

Cherry

customer feedback, routed to the team that should hear it

live ↗code ↗

Why I made it

An exercise in understanding the full feedback loop, from what a customer says to the internal team that acts on it. I pictured a company like Anthropic, with several products (Claude, Claude Code, Claude Design, Cowork), feedback arriving from everywhere, and a system that has to triage each piece to the right team.

What it does

The fun challenge was writing the evals: what counts as a credible piece of feedback, and which team it belongs to.
