01

Selected builds

Each build is a working answer to a problem teams hit when they deploy AI agents. A few patterns run through them, and they carry over to any workflow where a wrong answer is expensive:

Split the work
The model handles language and judgment; deterministic code handles math, rules, and facts.
Constrain the output
Forced function calls and validated schemas, so every answer arrives in a shape software can check.
Ground every claim
Answers cite their sources, and data is prepared ahead of time rather than fetched live.
Measure, don’t eyeball
Labeled eval sets and LLM-as-judge checks, plus model benchmarks on quality, latency, and cost.
Keep a human in the loop
Reviewer corrections are captured and fed back, so the system improves where it was wrong.

Delphi

Stock stories.

I built Delphi to give readers a pulse on the 100 most popular stocks on Robinhood. Markets and the world move each other, so knowing what the market is doing tells you a lot about what’s going on everywhere else.

Each company gets curated writing on its profile, context, competitive landscape, and what its latest earnings showed. Every story is sourced, and the site refreshes daily after the close.
