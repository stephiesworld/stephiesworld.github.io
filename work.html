Selected builds

Prototypes I design under Studio Felix: projects for AI adoption, each one made to train teams to work with agents.

Henry

Amazon 1P vendor operations.

Agentic tool-calling with web search for research; deterministic code for the financial math. Classifies chargebacks by root cause and drafts the dispute. Graded by evals.

tool useevalsdeterministic math

Delphi

Stock stories.

The 100 most popular stocks on Robinhood, explained the way a CEO would explain them: what the company is betting on, why it wins against its rivals, and what the latest earnings changed. Every story is sourced, and refreshes daily after the close.

Inspired by six years of listening to hedge fund analysts explain the market.

Cherry

Voice-of-customer triage.

Searches the open web for feedback, clusters it into issues, and ranks them by severity, then reach, then recency. Your corrections re-rank it.

clusteringrankinghuman-in-the-loop

Stanley

Calendar agent.

Reads the week’s schedule and proposes one-line fixes over Telegram, by voice. The write to the calendar is gated in code, so it never acts without confirmation.
