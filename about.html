About

I’m Stephie, a builder who has worked across a number of industries, and I get excited about systems thinking.

I’ve helped people save money and plan for the future, created body wash and razors across international markets, founded a company, and helped big brands grow on Amazon.

In my free time, I build things that help people easily find the information they need, particularly around the markets (Delphi), product feedback (Cherry), and white-glove e-commerce sales (Henry).

I’m also writing 29, a novel about an alien on Wall Street who slowly falls in love with the ordinary business of being human.

I love design, philosophy, music, and cities. I’m based in New York, but sometimes you’ll find me in Paris, London, or Shanghai.

Say hi if you want to talk about building with agents, know a company I should see, have a song for me, or want to hit on the court. I’m always up for coffee.
