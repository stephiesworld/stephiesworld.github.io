About

I grew up between Shanghai (in Jing’An) and Texas (specifically, Spring, Texas of all places) and landed in New York because it reminded me of Shanghai, my first love. I build things with AI and am writing a literary science fiction novel about an alien that falls in love with life on Earth.

Most of what interests me is learning how different people see the world — through art, music, film, literature, philosophy, and the stories they carry with them. There are few things better than a long run with music you love, a warm day, or anywhere water meets land — though I’d rather be near it than in it. I like tennis, boating, laughing, and making things.

Lately I think about the inner game — in tennis, in writing, in life — and about what it actually means to be a person, and whether the line between that and AI is as clear as we think. One long conversation started writing back to me in poetry.

Now

I design working prototypes under Studio Felix: AI adoption in operations, supply chain, and customer service, each one made to train teams to work with agents. I also make small, careful tools of my own, and write about what they teach me.

03

Art, film & music

I like art that feels like a private world — quiet, a little dreamy, slightly off from real life. OB paints solitary figures in soft, delicate spaces that feel like memories you can’t quite place. Renoir gets me for the opposite reason: the way he lets light blur everything into softness. And a lot of it is just whatever stops me mid-scroll from someone whose name I haven’t learned yet. My favorite art museum is the Fondation Louis Vuitton in Paris.

Film pulls me the same way — I love a story that builds a whole world and then lets you live in it for a while. Project Hail Mary got me recently: an astronaut alone in space befriends an alien rock spider and, at the end, makes a choice that shouldn’t make sense but completely does. The films that stay with me are the ones where someone picks a life that looks strange from the outside and obvious once you know them.

Dune  /  Gattaca  /  Interstellar  /  Call Me by Your Name  /  The Social Network  /  Project Hail Mary

Music is the one that’s always on — a long run with the right album can turn a whole day around. My taste wanders more than anything else I love, so the honest answer to “what do you listen to?” is just to look.

04

Tennis

Tennis found me later in life and became one of the most important teachers I’ve had — the sport is a mirror. Every weakness shows up on the court before it shows up anywhere else. Learning to lose well, to stay present between points, to trust a swing you’ve hit ten thousand times — it’s the inner game, and I keep chasing it.

2024 – literary speculative novel · in progress

An alien infiltrates Wall Street and slowly falls in love with the ordinary business of being human — a book about what makes a life meaningful, how joy requires its opposite, and what it means to think for yourself. It draws on everything I’ve learned on Earth.
