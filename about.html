About

I’m Stephie. I grew up between Shanghai and Texas, and I live in New York.

I love building with AI, like this site and a few other projects. Along the way, I’ve worked with Amazon’s largest sellers, founded a dumpling company, run global R&D programs at Harry’s, and built AI for wealth management at Morgan Stanley. The thread through all of it is how things actually get made, and where people’s judgment hides inside them.

I’m also writing 29, a novel about an alien on Wall Street who slowly falls in love with the ordinary business of being human. Between the novel and the work, I keep circling the same question: what makes a person a person, and is the line between us and artificial intelligence as clear as we think?

What stays with me, in books and in people, is someone choosing a life that looks strange from the outside and obvious once you know them.

Say hi if you want to talk about building with agents, know a company I should see, have a song for me, or want to hit on the court. I’m always up for coffee.
