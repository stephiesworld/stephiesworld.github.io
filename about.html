About

I’m Stephie. I grew up between Shanghai and Texas and live in New York City. Most days I’m somewhere in AI: reading about it, trying the new agents, or building something in Claude.

I’m also writing 29, a novel about an alien who infiltrates Wall Street and slowly falls in love with the ordinary business of being human. Between the two, I keep coming back to what it means to be a person, and whether the line between that and artificial intelligence is as clear as we think.

What stays with me, in books, films, and people, is someone choosing a life that looks strange from the outside and obvious once you know them.
